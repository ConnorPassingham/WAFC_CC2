Tournament Overview

Three days. One club.

01

Three Days of Games

Saturday and Sunday group play, followed by finals day on Monday at John Kerkhof Park, Cambridge.

02

Competitive Football

A chance for our teams to test themselves against clubs from around New Zealand in a quality tournament environment.

03

Player Development

The weekend is about learning, adapting and growing together. Performance and development come before the scoreboard.

Before You Arrive

Gameday Expectations

A consistent routine helps every team prepare properly.

−60

Arrive one hour before kick-off

Meet your coach, check in and gather with the rest of your squad.

−45

Team warm-up begins

Players should be ready to start the team warm-up 45 minutes before kick-off.

00

Kick-off

Be prepared, focused and ready to represent Warkworth AFC.

Arrive in full required gear: playing shirt, alternative shirt as a spare, club shorts, club socks, boots and shinpads.

Pack both playing shirts every day so teams are ready for any colour clash.

Main Kit

Warkworth AFC red playing shirt

02

Alternative Kit

Warkworth AFC black playing shirt

03

Shorts & Socks

Black Warkworth AFC Adidas shorts + black/grey club Adidas socks

04

Footwear & Safety

Football boots + shinpads are required

Extras

Players are welcome to wear headbands and undershirts.

Not Permitted

No watches, jewellery or hats during games, except goalkeeper headwear where permitted.

Goalkeepers

Green shirt as main kit, yellow shirt as alternative. Bring gloves and long sports pants if preferred.

How We Represent Warkworth

Our Values

The way we behave matters as much as the football we play.

Players
  • RespectTeammates, opponents, officials and coaches.
  • AttentionListen, learn and stay engaged.
  • Work Rate & EffortGive your best in every moment.
  • AdaptabilityBe ready to play different positions and take on new challenges.
Parents
  • No Touchline CoachingLet the players make decisions and the coaches coach.
  • Praise Effort, Not ResultsRecognise attitude, bravery and improvement.
  • Growth Over WinningDevelopment is the priority.
  • Respect All RolesPlayers play, coaches coach, officials officiate, parents support.
